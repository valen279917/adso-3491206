08-Relative Units

Specify a length relative to another length property.

  • Pixels (px)
  • M (em)
  • x (ex)
  • Root M (rem)
  • Viewport Units (vh)
  • Viewport Units (vw)
  • Large Viewport (lv|1vw)
  • Small Viewport (sv|1vw)
  • Dinamic Viewport (dv|dvw)

  • px: 15px
  • em: 1.5em
  • ex: 2ex
  • rem: 2.4rem
